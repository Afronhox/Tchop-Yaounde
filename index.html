About Tchop Yaoundé

Tchop Yaoundé is a student project. It helps people find affordable places to eat in Yaoundé by searching, filtering, and checking sample menus.

How it works

  1. Search a restaurant, area, or dish.
  2. Filter by budget, food type, or quartier.
  3. Open a menu to see dishes and prices.
  4. Save places you like with the heart button.

Many places and prices are sample data for the demo. Always confirm with the restaurant before you go.
